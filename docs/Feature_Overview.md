# CampusOS – Feature Overview

---

## Authentication

- **Login** – Secure login for Super Admin, Admin, Warden, and Student using email/ID and password.
- **Forgot Password** – Password reset using email verification or OTP.
- **Change Password** – Users can securely update their password.
- **Role‑Based Access** – Access control for Super Admin, Admin, Warden, Student, Library Admin, Inventory Admin, Library Staff, and Inventory Staff.

---

## Database

- **Student Database** – Store student personal, academic, hostel, and contact information.
- **Hostel Database** – Store hostel details, hostel blocks, and capacities.
- **Room Database** – Manage room details, occupancy, room type, and availability.
- **Fee Database** – Store fee records, payment history, due dates, and receipts.
- **Complaint Database** – Store complaints, complaint status, priority, and resolutions.
- **Visitor Database** – Record visitor details, approval status, and visit history.
- **Attendance Database** – Store daily hostel attendance records.

---

## Super Admin

- **Admin Management** – Add, edit, delete, and manage admin accounts.
- **Hostel Management** – Create and manage hostels.
- **User Management** – Manage hostel, library, and inventory admin users.

---

## Admin

- **Student Management** – Add, edit, delete, and search student records.
- **Room Allocation** – Allocate rooms and beds to students.
- **Staff Management** – Manage wardens and view staff details.
- **Fee Management** – Define fee structure and update fee status.
- **Complaint Management** – View, assign, monitor, approve, or reject complaints.
- **Notice Board** – Create and publish notices for students.
- **Reports & Analytics** – View hostel reports, occupancy, revenue, and complaint analytics.

---

## Warden

- **Dashboard** – View hostel occupancy, recent in/out records, complaints, and notices.
- **Leave Application** – View and approve leave requests.
- **Room Inspection** – Record room inspections and maintenance status.
- **Room Allocation** – Allocate or update room occupancy.
- **Room Change** – Approve or reject room change requests.
- **Staff Management** – Add, edit, delete, and manage hostel staff details.
- **Furniture Management** – Track hostel furniture and room assets.
- **Visitor Management** – Approve visitor requests and maintain visitor logs.
- **Complaint Tracking** – Update complaint status and assign maintenance staff.
- **Notice Management** – Publish hostel‑specific notices.
- **Mess Management** – Update daily menu and customized meal timings.
- **Student Records** – View student profiles and hostel information.
- **Emergency Contacts** – View parent and emergency contact details of students.

---

## Student

- **Dashboard** – View room details, in/out records, fees, complaints, and notices.
- **Profile Management** – View and update personal profile.
- **Room Details** – View room number, bed number, roommate details, and hostel rules.
- **In/Out Sheet** – View entry and exit history with date and time records.
- **Leave Management** – Apply for leave and track approval status.
- **Fee Management** – View fee status, payment history, download receipts, and make online/offline fee payments.
- **Complaint Management** – Raise complaints and track complaint resolution.
- **Visitor Management** – Request visitor approval and view visitor history.
- **Mess Management** – View daily menu and meal timings.
- **Notice Board** – View announcements and hostel notices.
- **Room Change** – Submit room change requests and monitor request status.
- **Emergency Contacts** – View warden, security, and emergency contact details.

---

## Security

- **In/Out Sheet** – Maintain student entry and exit records.
- **Scanner** – Scan student ID card using Barcode/QR Code for verification.

---

## Reports (Admin & Warden)

- **Occupancy Report** – Hostel and room occupancy statistics.
- **Fee Report** – Fee collection, pending fees, and payment summaries.
- **Complaint Report** – Complaint count, resolution status, and response time.
- **Attendance Report** – Student attendance summaries.
- **Visitor Report** – Visitor logs and approval history.

---

## Notifications

- **System Notifications** – Notifications for fees, complaints, notices, leave approvals, room changes, visitor approvals, and other important updates.

---

## Search & Filter

- **Search Records** – Search students, rooms, complaints, visitors, and payments.
- **Filter Data** – Filter records based on hostel, room, status, department, and date.

---

*This document consolidates the functional modules, associated dashboards, and the primary user roles that will interact with each feature.*
